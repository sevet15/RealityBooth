Privacy Policy for RealityBooth

Last updated: September 1, 2026

RealityBooth ("we", "our", or "the App"), developed by Steven Valentino, is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines how our application operates and our strict commitment to zero data collection.

1. Zero Personal Data Collection

RealityBooth does NOT collect, track, store, or transmit any personal identifiable information, device identifiers, or analytics. The app functions completely offline and all 3D rendering and RealityKit computations are performed locally on your device.

2. Device Permissions & Usage

RealityBooth requests only the minimum device permissions necessary to provide augmented reality visualization:

  • Camera Access: Used exclusively to run Apple ARKit and RealityKit sessions for surface detection and 3D model placement. Camera frames are processed strictly in real-time volatile memory (RAM) and are never recorded, analyzed for biometrics, or sent to any server.
  • Photo Library Access: Requested only when you explicitly tap the camera shutter button to save an AR snapshot directly to your local device. We do not access or read your existing photos.

3. Third-Party Services & Tracking

RealityBooth does not include any third-party tracking SDKs, advertising networks, or background telemetry. No user data is ever sold or shared.
