Thank you for using Real ini. We are here to help you get the best experience visualizing 3D models in real-world scale.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I import my own 3D files?

Tap the Add Model button, then tap the + button to open the iOS Files picker. You can import .USDZ, .USDC, or .REALITY files directly from iCloud Drive, local storage, or AirDrop.

Why does the app need camera access?

Real ini uses Apple ARKit and RealityKit to scan your room surfaces (floors, tables, desks) and place 3D models at true 1:1 scale. The camera feed is processed strictly in local RAM and is never recorded or transmitted.

How do I slide a model across the floor?

Simply place your finger on any placed 3D model and drag it across the surface. The model will smoothly follow your finger in real time at 60 FPS.
