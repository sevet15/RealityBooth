Engineered for Spatial Accuracy

Every feature built with millimeter precision and fluid real-time responsiveness.

📏

Apple HIG 1:1 Measurements

Live floating dimension callouts follow Apple HIG sequence: Width × Length × Height in centimeters (W × L × H cm) with 5% to 500% zoom.

🪵

Precision LiDAR Grounding

Advanced container-space raycasting anchors models flat on floors, tables, and desks at ground level (y = 0.0m) with zero floating.

👆

1-Finger Surface Sliding

Touch any placed 3D model and glide it smoothly across the surface at 60 FPS. No repetitive single-tap positioning needed.

💡

Scale-Consistent Studio Lighting

Multi-directional soft-white studio lighting automatically regulates intensity as you scale, preventing glare or dimming.

📳

Stepped Tactile Haptics

Feel physical ticks every 5% scale increment, with a distinct firm notch when snapping to exact 100% 1:1 true scale.

🔄

Lag-Free 100% Reset

Instantly restore original 1:1 scale and neutral orientation with a single tap, accompanied by smooth loading feedback.
